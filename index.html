Work @ i14y Lab

During my one-year experience at i14y Lab, Deutsche Telekom, a consortium focused on Open Radio Networks, both as internship and working student, I developed several technical demos: Augmented Reality, Physical-Digital interfaces, Touch Screens, animation...

I was able to develop skills in different creative approaches that fit the assignment—using game engines, web design, 3D rendering, motion, animation, and video editing.

Overall purpose: bring the topic of Open Radio Access closer to the audience. This inspired my Bachelor Thesis.

Extended Reality

O-Ran Mixed Reality Experience

Components Sandbox

In my Bachelor’s project, I brought complex technical processes to life through mixed reality—letting users not just learn, but explore and interact with network components. With experience from my internship at i14y Lab in Berlin, I created an immersive prototype using Unity and the Meta SDK, blending symbolic metaphors with hands-on interaction scenarios.

Users navigate gesture-based interactions across layered environments, supported by visual cues and narrative design inspired by cyberpunk aesthetics. Early prototypes and user tests highlighted MR’s power to make complex systems both understandable and emotionally engaging. This thesis shows how XR storytelling can transform niche technologies into accessible, immersive experiences.

Other Projects

Optimize_ The Workshop

Within this participatory workshop on "Societal Pressure for Self-Optimization", we explored together the causes and dynamics of the pressure to constantly improve ourselves.

We then co-created ideas for fostering an environment that supports healthy personal growth. In collaboration with Annika Ehritt, Yette Shira Strauss Suhr, Luka Saje.

Birdie Sound Board

Birdie is a conceptualized musical instrument and social game
designed to support physical mobility in seniors.

It uses tactile modules and playful interaction to promote movement, cognition, and a sense of safety. Designed as a submission for the Rimowa Design Award, which I was honored to be chosen as an participant. Created in collaboration with Viktor Jarek.
